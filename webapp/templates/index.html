Method

Dataset

PlantVillage — segmented specimens, tomato / potato / corn, 17 conditions, 24,164 images.

Classical instruments

Random Forest & K-Nearest Neighbours, trained on colour, texture (GLCM), and shape (Hu moment) measurements.

Learned instrument

ResNet50, transfer-learned from ImageNet, fine-tuned on the final residual block.
